How Hydra Publisher works

The system assists with preparation and transfer. It does not remove the creator from the publishing decision.

1

Prepare original videos

Hydra Publisher helps creators organize captions, metadata, scheduling information, and the final short-form video file.

2

Review before transfer

Videos remain under creator review until the creator explicitly approves the content for transfer or publication.

3

Transfer approved content

Once approved, Hydra Publisher can transfer the creator’s original video and associated metadata to supported platform workflows.

4

Finish inside TikTok

TikTok uploads enter the creator’s TikTok inbox so the creator can edit the video, choose native audio, review the post, and publish it from TikTok.

Creators explicitly approve uploads.

Hydra Publisher is designed around creator control. Content is not considered approved merely because it has been generated, rendered, or added to a queue.

  • The creator reviews the final video.
  • The creator approves the upload or transfer.
  • TikTok editing and publication are completed by the creator.
  • Creators can stop or revise the process before publication.
