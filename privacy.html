Overview

Hydra Publisher helps creators prepare and transfer original short-form videos to supported publishing workflows.

Information processed

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, Hydra Publisher may process account identifiers, access tokens, video files, captions, descriptions, scheduling data, publishing status, and published post identifiers or URLs.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the creator, prepare approved content, transfer videos and metadata, monitor publishing status, and report results or errors.

Creator control

Creators explicitly approve uploads. TikTok uploads enter the creator’s inbox for editing and publication by the creator.

Data sharing

Data is shared only with platforms and service providers needed to perform the creator-requested workflow. Hydra Publisher does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Workflow data is retained only as long as needed for publishing, troubleshooting, audit records, or legal obligations. Creators may request deletion or revoke platform access.

Security

Access credentials should be stored in secured credential systems and must not be placed in public repositories or public pages.
