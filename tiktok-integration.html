TikTok Integration

Creator-controlled publishing from Hydra Publisher to TikTok

Hydra Publisher helps authorized creators review approved short-form videos, connect their own TikTok account, upload a video to their TikTok inbox, complete native editing in TikTok, and monitor publication status.

Hydra Publisher does not publish a TikTok inbox upload without creator action. The creator reviews the uploaded video inside TikTok and decides whether to publish it.

What the integration does

Account authorization

Creators connect TikTok through TikTok Login Kit. Hydra Publisher receives only the permissions approved during TikTok's authorization process.

Inbox upload

Approved videos are transferred through TikTok's Content Posting API and delivered to the creator's TikTok inbox for final review and editing.

Native editing

The creator may add an eligible TikTok sound, adjust volume, confirm the caption, select visibility, choose a cover, and make other TikTok-native edits.

Status monitoring

Hydra Publisher checks the TikTok publication state and records the final status and available public post information after the creator publishes.

Creator workflow

  1. Connect TikTok The creator authorizes Hydra Publisher through TikTok's OAuth consent screen.
  2. Review approved content The creator selects a video that has passed the application's review process.
  3. Upload to TikTok The video is uploaded through TikTok's Content Posting API.
  4. Open the TikTok notification TikTok places the uploaded video in the creator's inbox for final action.
  5. Edit and publish in TikTok The creator reviews the post, applies permitted native editing options, and publishes.
  6. Confirm publication status Hydra Publisher checks the result and updates the creator's publishing record.

TikTok permissions used

Scope Purpose
user.info.basic Displays basic connected-account information so the creator can confirm which TikTok account is authorized.
video.upload Uploads an approved video to the connected creator's TikTok inbox for final review, editing, and publication.
video.list Checks published-video information and retrieves available post metadata, such as the TikTok video identifier or share URL.

Data handling

  • Hydra Publisher does not ask creators for their TikTok password.
  • TikTok authorization occurs on TikTok's own consent screen.
  • OAuth tokens are used only to perform actions authorized by the connected creator.
  • Temporary TikTok upload URLs are not published or shown on public pages.
  • Hydra Publisher does not sell creator data or provide it to advertisers.
  • Creators can disconnect TikTok and revoke access at any time.

Additional details are available in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Disconnecting TikTok

Creators can disconnect TikTok from Hydra Publisher's connection console. They may also revoke application access from their TikTok account settings. After disconnection, Hydra Publisher stops making authorized TikTok API requests for that account.
